# Terms of Service

_Template for the Nellai Green & Civic platform — review before publishing
as a live policy for a production deployment._

**Last updated:** 2026-09-18

## What this platform is

Nellai Green & Civic lets citizens report civic and environmental issues,
lets designated authorities track and resolve them, and lets volunteers
organize environmental activities. It is a reporting and coordination tool
-- **it is not an emergency service**. For a life-threatening emergency,
contact local emergency services directly, not this app.

## Acceptable use

You agree not to:

- Submit knowingly false reports
- Submit content that is abusive, harassing, or violates others' privacy
- Attempt to access another user's account or data
- Use the API outside its documented rate limits to disrupt the service

Accounts found to be filing deliberately false or abusive reports may be
deactivated.

## Content you submit

By submitting a complaint (description, photo, location), you grant the
platform operator the right to display it to the relevant authority, on the
public map (without your personal details, per the Privacy Policy), and to
retain it as part of the civic record described in the Privacy Policy.

## Land ownership claims (Green Nellai / afforestation module)

The platform's afforestation features never assert that land is
government-owned based on satellite imagery or map data alone (see spec
section 17 / `modules/afforestation/backend/models.py`). Any ownership
status shown is either `UNVERIFIED` or has been explicitly confirmed by an
administrator against official records. Do not rely on this platform as
proof of land ownership for any legal purpose.
